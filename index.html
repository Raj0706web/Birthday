A little story — just for you
Chapter One
There was a little girl, just figuring out the world.
Quiet. Curious. Entirely her own.
Growing up
She grew up without needing anyone to complete her.
Simple days. Simple joy. No drama. No chaos.
School days
She had her world — her little routines,
her silences, her unbothered peace.
Teenage years
No one could touch her calm.
Nonchalant. Unbothered. Completely herself.
20 years of this
Twenty whole years. Solo. Clear-headed.
Not waiting for anyone. Not needing anyone.
And then —
She was doing perfectly fine, actually.
Life was calm, clean, and hers.
And then… someone showed up.
Enter: the chaos
Suddenly there was someone who thought
her silence was a challenge to fill.
Annoying? Absolutely. Gone? Never.
The awkward era
He made things awkward. On purpose, probably.
Argued about things that didn't matter.
Won nothing. Showed up anyway.
The possessive one
Possessive? Guilty.
Doesn't share her attention well.
Still figuring that part out.
But —
He loves you in the most embarrassingly obvious way.
Adores you quietly when you're not looking.
Yours. Fully. Stubbornly yours.
♡
You spent 20 years being perfectly okay alone.
Which means this isn't need —
it's a choice.

And he chooses you. Every single, annoying day.
Happy Birthday, Mohor ♡
